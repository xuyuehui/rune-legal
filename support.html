Frequently asked questions

I tapped "Watch Ad" and it says the ad is loading, or is unavailable.

Ads are supplied by ad networks and availability depends on your region, your connection, and demand at that moment. If no ad is available, the button may say the ad is unavailable. Try again later; no progress is lost.

Why does the game ask to track me?

iOS asks for permission before an app may use your advertising identifier. Allowing it lets ads be more relevant, which supports keeping the game free. Declining is fine — the game works the same. You can change the answer in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Is my progress saved? Can I move it to another device?

Progress is stored locally on your device. Because the game has no account system, progress does not transfer between devices or survive deleting the app.

Can I play offline?

The game is designed to be playable without a connection. Ads require a connection and simply will not be offered when you are offline.

How do I reset my progress?

Deleting the app and reinstalling clears local progress. This cannot be undone.
