Rune Blast — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Rune Blast ("the Game", "we", "us") collects, why we collect it, and what choices you have. The Game is published for iOS under bundle identifier com.duoguagua.RuneBlastGlobal.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or postal address.

CategoryWhat it isPurpose
Usage data Game events such as when a level starts or ends, whether a run was won or lost, how many floors were cleared, and when a rewarded ad was shown. Understanding how the Game is played so we can fix problems and improve it.
Diagnostics and device data App version, device model, operating system version, language, and a randomly generated app instance identifier. Crash diagnosis, compatibility tuning, and aggregated retention reporting.
Advertising data If you allow tracking, the advertising identifier (IDFA) of your device; if you do not, a non-personalized identifier. Also IP address and ad-interaction events (impression, reward, close). Serving rewarded video ads, measuring them, and preventing fraud.

3. Advertising and tracking permission

The Game shows optional rewarded video ads. On first entering the game you will be asked whether the Game may track you across other companies' apps and websites (Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt).

If you decline, the Game still works and ads may still be shown, but they cannot be personalized using your advertising identifier. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your device.

4. Legal bases (EEA and UK)

  • Legitimate interests — operating, securing, and improving the Game.
  • Consent — personalized advertising and any tracking across apps and websites, which you give or withhold through the tracking prompt.

5. How long we keep data

Event-level analytics data is retained by our analytics provider for the period configured on our account, currently up to 14 months, after which it is aggregated or deleted. Advertising data is retained according to the ad provider's own retention schedule.

6. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Tracking: turn off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Advertising identifier: you may reset it in your device settings.
  • Access, deletion, and objection (EEA/UK GDPR, California CCPA/CPRA): email us and we will respond within the period required by applicable law.
  • Do Not Sell/Share: we do not sell personal information. Personalized advertising is treated as "sharing" under the CPRA and is controlled by the tracking prompt described above.

8. Data transfers

Our service providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Transfers are covered by the providers' standard contractual arrangements.

9. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and, where appropriate, notify you in the Game.
